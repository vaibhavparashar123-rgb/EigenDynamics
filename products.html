Technical Focus Areas

What Goes Into a Great Flight Controller

These are the engineering domains we are investing in to build controllers that are genuinely world-class.

🧠

Sensor Fusion

Advanced algorithms combining data from IMU, barometer, GPS, magnetometer, and optical flow for precise state estimation in real-world conditions.

⚡

Real-Time Control

High-frequency control loops running on purpose-designed hardware — ensuring responsive, stable flight even in challenging conditions like Indian weather and terrain.

🔒

Fail-Safe Architecture

Redundancy and fail-safe systems are built into the design from day one — not added as an afterthought. Safety is non-negotiable.

📡

Communication Protocols

Support for standard and custom telemetry, ground control station integration, and encrypted communication for sensitive applications.

🛠️

Configurable Firmware

Adaptable firmware architecture to support varied airframe types, payloads, and mission profiles — from precision agriculture to surveillance.

🏭

Made in India Hardware

Not just software on imported boards — we're working toward hardware designed and assembled in India, with indigenous supply chains wherever possible.

🤝

If you're a drone operator, researcher, defence organisation, or fellow startup who wants to be part of what we're building — we'd love to hear from you. Early partnerships shape the product.
