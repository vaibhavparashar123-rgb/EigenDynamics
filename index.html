Our Mission

Precision Engineering
for Indian Skies

India's unmanned aerial systems sector is growing rapidly — and it needs indigenous, reliable flight controllers that match and exceed global standards. EigenDynamics is building exactly that, right here in Faridabad.


We believe India shouldn't have to depend on foreign technology for its skies. Our mission is to design, engineer, and deliver flight control systems built in India — for defence, commercial, research, and hobbyist applications alike.
