Our Story

An Idea That Took Flight

EigenDynamics started as an idea — that India deserves world-class, indigenous flight controllers for its unmanned aerial systems. We're turning that idea into reality, right here in Faridabad.

Where We Come From

From Idea to Reality

EigenDynamics was born from a simple but powerful observation: India's drone and UAV industry is growing at an extraordinary pace, yet the critical flight control hardware at the heart of these systems is overwhelmingly imported.


We set out to change that. Based in Sector 65, Faridabad — right in the industrial heartland of Haryana — we are a young, technically driven startup with a clear mission: to design and manufacture specialised flight controllers that are wholly conceived, engineered, and built in India.


We are at the beginning of our journey. We have not yet made tall claims — and we won't. What we have is deep conviction, growing technical capability, and an unwavering belief in what Indian engineering can achieve.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To develop specialised, high-reliability flight controllers for unmanned aerial systems — built entirely in India, solving real Indian problems, and reducing the sector's dependence on imported hardware.

🔭

Our Vision

A future where Indian skies are controlled by Indian technology — where every drone, every UAV, every unmanned aircraft that flies over this country carries flight intelligence engineered here, by us, for us.

What We Stand For

Our Values

We're a startup, which means resources are finite — but our principles are not. These are the values that guide every decision we make.

Indigenous Innovation

We build in India, for India. Not just as a tagline — as an operating principle. Every component, every design decision, every line of firmware is evaluated through the lens of what India needs.

Technical Honesty

We are early-stage. We will not claim capabilities we don't yet have. Our credibility is built on being straight with our partners, customers, and community — always.

Precision Over Speed

Flight controllers sit at the heart of safety-critical systems. We move fast where we can, but we will never compromise on engineering rigour where it matters most.

Open Collaboration

India's aerospace ecosystem grows when we grow together. We actively seek to collaborate with research institutions, defence organisations, and fellow startups.
